# AI Center of Excellence (CoE) Governance

## 1. Mandate of the AI Center of Excellence

An **AI Center of Excellence (CoE)** does NOT build every AI application. Its primary mission is to **eliminate duplicated effort, establish enterprise golden paths, enforce compliance, and aggregate purchasing power**.

### Core Deliverables of the CoE
1. **The Enterprise AI Gateway**: Central access point for all foundation models.
2. **Standardized Evaluation Frameworks**: Automated CI/CD testing pipelines and golden datasets.
3. **Reusable RAG Accelerators**: Reusable Terraform modules for hybrid search and vector databases.
4. **Architectural Review Gating**: Mandatory checkpoint before any AI capability enters production.
