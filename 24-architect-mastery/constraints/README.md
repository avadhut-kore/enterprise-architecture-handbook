Architecture is the art of achieving maximum business impact within unyielding physical, legal, and organizational boundaries.

## Core Rules
1. **Never fight physics**: Network latency, speed of light in fiber (~5ms per 1,000km round-trip), and disk I/O cannot be negotiated.
2. **Clarify Real vs Perceived Constraints**: Many enterprise constraints are folklore rather than factual limits.
