# Architecture Business Case Template

Use this comprehensive template when proposing major enterprise replatforming, cloud migrations, or multi-year digital transformation programs.

## 1. Executive Summary
- Concise statement of problem, solution, financial metrics (ROI, NPV, Payback Period).

## 2. Strategic Context & Alignment
- Mapping to enterprise strategic pillars (e.g., international expansion, operational resilience).

## 3. Financial Analysis (TCO & ROI)
```
Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Comparison:
Year 0: Implementation (Staff, Consulting, Migration)
Year 1-3: Operating Costs (Cloud spend, Licensing, Maintenance)
Net Present Value (NPV) calculated at 8% discount rate.
```

## 4. Architectural Alternatives Evaluated
- **Option 1: Status Quo (Do Nothing)** — Explicitly document Cost of Inaction (CoI).
- **Option 2: Lift-and-Shift to Cloud** — Low risk, moderate cost savings, zero agility improvement.
- **Option 3: Modern Cloud-Native Event-Driven Architecture (Recommended)** — Highest agility, optimal long-term TCO.

## 5. Implementation Roadmap & Milestones
- Phase 1: Foundation & Seams (Months 1-3)
- Phase 2: Pilot Migration & Canary (Months 4-6)
- Phase 3: Full Cutover & Legacy Decommissioning (Months 7-12)

## 6. Risk Matrix and Governance Sign-off
- Comprehensive risk register with mitigation owners.
