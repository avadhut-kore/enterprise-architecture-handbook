# Role Readiness Gate: Enterprise Architect (EA)

> **"Readiness for Enterprise Architect is proven by business capability alignment, application portfolio rationalization, IT capital allocation rigor, and corporate digital transformation leadership."**

---

## 1. Readiness Threshold Matrix

| Pillar | Required Standard | Verification Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Knowledge** | Level 5 (Strategic/Enterprise) in Business Acumen, Governance, and Strategy; Level 3–4 across Core Technical Domains. | Review against [Competency Matrix](../skill-matrix/architect-competency-matrix.md). |
| **2. Experience** | Led an enterprise application rationalization initiative auditing 50+ applications; retired systems saving $1M+ annually; contributed to M&A technical due diligence. | Executive sponsorship signoff, financial audit reports, and IT budget variance. |
| **3. Decisions** | Authored enterprise architecture principles; formulated multi-year IT capital investment business cases (NPV/IRR). | Corporate policy repository and finance committee records. |
| **4. Evidence** | 1 Enterprise Business Capability Map; 1 Application Portfolio TIME Scorecard; 1 Multi-Year Modernization Roadmap. | Enterprise Architecture repository artifacts. |

---

## 2. Core Readiness Checklist

### Business Architecture & Strategy Alignment
- [ ] Models hierarchical Business Capability Maps (Level 1–3) directly mapped to strategic corporate revenue drivers ([Capability Worksheet](../../21-architecture-tools/capability-mapping-worksheet.md)).
- [ ] Fluently communicates with CFOs, CIOs, and Business Unit Presidents without resorting to technical jargon.
- [ ] Evaluates technology business cases using Net Present Value (NPV), ROI, and 5-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).

### Application Portfolio Management (APM) & Rationalization
- [ ] Classifies large enterprise application landscapes using the TIME framework (Tolerate, Invest, Migrate, Eliminate) to eliminate redundant spend ([APM Guide](../../23-enterprise-architecture/README.md)).
- [ ] Authors modernization strategies for legacy mainframes, ERPs (SAP), and CRMs (Salesforce) using the 8R framework ([15-modernization](../../15-modernization/README.md)).
- [ ] Leads M&A technical due diligence, evaluating target architecture synergies, cyber liabilities, and integration debt.

### Enterprise Governance & Global Compliance
- [ ] Presides over the Enterprise Architecture Review Board (EARB); shifts governance from bureaucratic policing to value enablement.
- [ ] Establishes non-negotiable enterprise architecture principles formally adopted across all business units ([Principles](../../ARCHITECTURE-PRINCIPLES.md)).
- [ ] Ensures system landscapes comply with multi-jurisdiction data sovereignty laws (GDPR, HIPAA, Basel III, PCI-DSS).

---

## 3. Mandatory Evidence Portfolio Items
1. **Business Capability Map**: Hierarchical model of corporate business capabilities and technology maturity ([23-enterprise-architecture](../../23-enterprise-architecture/README.md)).
2. **Application Portfolio TIME Scorecard**: Systematic rationalization analysis resulting in quantifiable OpEx/CapEx savings ([APM Calculator](../../21-architecture-tools/application-tco-calculator.md)).
3. **Multi-Year Enterprise Modernization Roadmap**: Sequenced transition states from legacy core systems to modern cloud platforms.

---

## 4. Remediation Plan if Not Ready
* **If lacking business architecture skills**: Partner with a business unit head to map their core value streams and identify technology pain points.
* **If lacking portfolio rationalization experience**: Audit a cluster of 10–15 department tools; model their TCO and build a consolidation business case for the CIO.
