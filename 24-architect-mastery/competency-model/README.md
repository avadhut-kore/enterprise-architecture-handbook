# Architect Competency Model: The 16 Core Architectural Disciplines

> **"Architectural competence is not a single score; it is a synthesis across 16 core disciplines spanning technical depth, distributed systems, platform engineering, business acumen, and executive leadership."**

Welcome to the **Competency Model** directory inside [`24-architect-mastery/`](../README.md). This module contains detailed, behaviorally anchored playbooks for each of the 16 core competencies that define the modern Solution Architect, Technical Architect, and Enterprise Architect.

## 2. The 16 Competency Deep-Dive Playbooks

Every competency playbook contains a rigorous 8-part breakdown: Definition, Strategic Importance, L1–L5 Behavioral Progression, Practical Experiences, Evidence Requirements, Common Blind Spots, Repository Links, and Diagnostic Questions.

### Quadrant 1: Technical Depth & Systems Thinking
* **[1. Technical Depth & Runtime Internals](./technical-depth.md)** — Memory allocation, garbage collection algorithms, thread contention, and CPU/IO efficiency.
* **[2. System Design & Scalability](./system-design.md)** — Horizontal scale, multi-region active-active topologies, availability %, and capacity planning.
* **[3. Software Architecture & Modularity](./software-architecture.md)** — Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Clean/Hexagonal boundaries, and modularity.
* **[4. Distributed Systems & Consistency](./distributed-systems.md)** — CAP/PACELC trade-offs, consensus (Raft), Sagas, and split-brain resolution.

### Quadrant 2: Infrastructure, Platforms & Operations
* **[5. Cloud Architecture & FinOps](./cloud-architecture.md)** — Multi-cloud landing zones, FinOps unit economics, hybrid connectivity, and cloud repatriation.
* **[6. Data Architecture & Lakehouses](./data-architecture.md)** — Polyglot persistence, Lakehouse table formats (Iceberg/Delta), CDC pipelines, and Data Mesh.
* **[7. Integration & Messaging Architecture](./integration-architecture.md)** — API-led connectivity, Kafka event backbones, schema registries, and enterprise ERP/CRM fabrics.
* **[8. Security Architecture & Zero Trust](./security-architecture.md)** — Zero Trust perimeters, STRIDE threat modeling, mTLS, and corporate IAM.
* **[9. Observability & SRE](./observability-and-sre.md)** — OpenTelemetry distributed tracing, SLO error budgets, and blameless incident post-mortems.
* **[10. DevOps & Platform Engineering](./devops-and-platform-engineering.md)** — Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs), GitOps, paved roads, and DORA optimization.
* **[11. AI & GenAI Systems Architecture](./ai-architecture.md)** — Production LLM serving (vLLM/Triton), PagedAttention, hybrid search RAG, and AI security.

### Quadrant 3: Business Acumen & Strategy
* **[12. Business Acumen & Unit Economics](./business-acumen.md)** — Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), unit economics, capital allocation (NPV/ROI), and business cases.
* **[15. Architecture Governance & ARB](./governance.md)** — Lightweight risk-based governance, ARB charters, Technology Radar curation, and fitness functions.
* **[16. Strategic Thinking & Evolution](./strategic-thinking.md)** — 5–10 year horizon scanning, radical simplification, and M&A technical due diligence.

### Quadrant 4: Leadership & Executive Influence
* **[13. Leadership & Influence Without Authority](./leadership.md)** — Influence without authority, technical conflict resolution, peer mentorship, and engineering culture.
* **[14. Executive & Technical Communication](./communication.md)** — 1-page executive memos, C4 visual modeling, and boardroom presentations.
