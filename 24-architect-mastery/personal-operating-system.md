# The Personal Architect Operating System

A structured operating system for practicing enterprise architecture at the highest level of professional mastery.

## 1. Daily Rituals (Execution & Grounding)
- **Review Telemetry**: Inspect top 3 production service latency distributions (p99) and active incident boards.
- **Maintain Second Brain**: Log emergent patterns, decision notes, and rejected ideas in Markdown notes.
- **Clear Review Queue**: Timebox async RFC/ADR reviews to 45 minutes daily.

## 2. Weekly Rituals (Alignment & Synthesis)
- **Architecture Guild / Katas**: Host or participate in technical discussions with engineering leads.
- **FinOps & Cost Audit**: Inspect weekly cloud consumption trends, unallocated compute, and egress anomalies.
- **1-on-1 Mentoring**: Coach one senior engineer on architectural trade-offs and decision framing.

## 3. Monthly Rituals (Strategy & Governance)
- **Technology Radar Review**: Evaluate candidates moving between Assess, Trial, Adopt, and Hold.
- **ADR Retrospectives**: Inspect decisions made 6 months prior to calibrate predictions against reality.
- **Pre-Mortem & Chaos Drills**: Run one prospective failure simulation or chaos test.

## 4. Quarterly Rituals (Portfolio & C-Suite)
- **Capability Gap Audit**: Map enterprise business capabilities against application portfolio health (TIME matrix).
- **Executive Architecture Brief**: Deliver a 1-page strategic roadmap update to C-level stakeholders.
- **Technical Debt Paydown Review**: Verify that 20% engineering allocation is delivering measurable MTTR/DORA gains.
