This module provides systematic frameworks and mental models for making high-stakes, ambiguous, and long-lasting architecture decisions.

## Core Principles

1. **Velocity Matters**: Differentiate between irreversible (Type 1) and reversible (Type 2) decisions to prevent decision paralysis.
2. **Document the 'Why'**: Record rejected alternatives, constraints, and driving forces in Architectural Decision Records ([16-architecture-deliverables](../../16-architecture-deliverables/README.md)).
3. **Guard Against Biases**: Architecture failure is often cognitive rather than technical.
