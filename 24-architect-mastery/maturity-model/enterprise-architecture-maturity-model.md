# Enterprise Architecture Maturity Model

This maturity model assesses how effectively an organization designs, governs, and evolves its technology landscape.

## 1. The 5 Stages of Enterprise Architecture Maturity

| Stage | Governance | Tech Stack | Alignment | Failure Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Ad-Hoc / Chaos** | Zero governance; shadow IT; undocumented silos. | Sprawling zoo of random frameworks. | Zero; tech chosen on whim. | Finger-pointing; recurring Sev-1s. |
| **Stage 2: Bureaucratic Gatekeeping**| Ivory-tower ARB; 50-page docs; slow approvals. | Rigid standards mandated from top. | Forced paper compliance. | Blame-heavy post-mortems; slow fixes. |
| **Stage 3: Standardized Delivery** | Pragmatic ADRs; lightweight peer review. | Curated Tech Radar; Golden Paths emerging. | Strategic roadmaps aligned to business. | Blameless retrospectives; runbooks. |
| **Stage 4: Automated Guardrails** | CI/CD fitness functions; automated compliance. | Internal Developer Platform (IDP) self-service. | High business agility; stream-aligned teams. | Automated canary rollbacks; game days. |
| **Stage 5: Evolutionary Mastery** | Continuous experimentation; real-options mindset. | Modular, loosely coupled, event-driven meshes. | Tech drives and opens new business models. | Chaos engineering; zero Sev-1 repeat failures. |
