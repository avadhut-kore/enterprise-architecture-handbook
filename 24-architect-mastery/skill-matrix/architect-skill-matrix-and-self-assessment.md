# Architect Skill Matrix and Self-Assessment

A complete architect combines deep technical competence with sharp business judgment, leadership influence, and operational rigor.

## 1. The 4-Quadrant Architecture Skill Model

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ QUADRANT 1: TECHNICAL DEPTH          │ QUADRANT 2: BUSINESS & STRATEGY      │
│ - Distributed systems theory         │ - Business capability modeling       │
│ - Data consistency & CAP trade-offs  │ - FinOps & unit cost economics       │
│ - Networking, OS & cloud internals   │ - Wardley mapping & buy vs build     │
│ - Security threat modeling & crypto  │ - M&A technical due diligence        │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ QUADRANT 3: LEADERSHIP & INFLUENCE   │ QUADRANT 4: OPERATIONAL RIGOR        │
│ - Executive communication (C-Suite)  │ - Production readiness reviews (PRR) │
│ - Resolving technical conflict       │ - Incident post-mortem leadership    │
│ - Storytelling & narrative framing   │ - Observability & telemetry design   │
│ - Mentoring & multiplying engineers  │ - Chaos engineering & red-teaming    │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Diagnostic Self-Assessment Scoring (1-5 Scale)
- **Level 1 (Awareness)**: Understands core definitions.
- **Level 2 (Application)**: Can build components under guidance.
- **Level 3 (Proficiency)**: Designs independent production systems.
- **Level 4 (Mastery)**: Guides multi-team architectures and navigates edge-case failure modes.
- **Level 5 (Enterprise Authority)**: Shapes enterprise strategy, mentors principals, and drives industry-wide innovation.
