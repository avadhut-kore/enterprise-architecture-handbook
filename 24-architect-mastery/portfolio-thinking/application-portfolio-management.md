# Application Portfolio Management (APM) & The TIME Framework

Enterprises accumulate hundreds of redundant applications over decades. Architects use portfolio management to maximize business value and eliminate waste.

## 1. The Gartner TIME Model for Architects

```
Business Value
     ▲
High │   [TOLERATE]               │   [INVEST]
     │   High Technical Debt,     │   Modern Stack,
     │   High Business Value      │   Strategic Core Differentiator
     │   -> Target for Refactor   │   -> Fund & Expand
     ├────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────
Low  │   [ELIMINATE]              │   [MIGRATE]
     │   Obsolete, Redundant,     │   Valuable capability trapped
     │   Low Business Utility     │   in unmaintainable platform
     │   -> Decommission ASAP     │   -> Shift to SaaS / Cloud
     └────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────►
         Low                      High
                   Technical Quality / Health
```

## 2. Portfolio Rationalization Rules
- **One Capability, One Platform**: Avoid maintaining three distinct messaging buses or four CRM systems across different business units.
- **Allocate 20% Capacity to Technical Debt**: Top-performing engineering organizations mandate that 20% of every sprint backlog is reserved for debt paydown and architectural evolution.
