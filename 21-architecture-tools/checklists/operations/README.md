# Enterprise Operational & SRE Checklists (`checklists/operations/`)

## Executive Summary

These checklists provide the operational governance gates used to verify production readiness, disaster recoverability, incident response maturity, and site reliability engineering standards.

---

## Operational Checklists Index
- [`production-readiness-review.md`](production-readiness-review.md) - Complete PRR gate before customer traffic launch.
- [`operational-readiness-review.md`](operational-readiness-review.md) - Service ownership, on-call, and runbook readiness.
- [`sre-readiness-review.md`](sre-readiness-review.md) - Golden signals, SLI/SLO definitions, and error budget policies.
- [`incident-readiness-review.md`](incident-readiness-review.md) - Severity matrix, Incident Commander, and communication templates.
- [`dr-readiness-review.md`](dr-readiness-review.md) - RTO/RPO alignment, cross-region failover, and game day verification.
- [`backup-recovery-review.md`](backup-recovery-review.md) - Immutable WORM backups and automated restore drill verification.
