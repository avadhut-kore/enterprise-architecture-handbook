# Checklist: Operational Readiness Review Checklist

## Executive Summary
This operational checklist must be validated prior to production promotion.

---

## Verification Criteria
- [ ] Single named engineering squad assigned as service owner.
- [ ] Primary and secondary on-call engineers scheduled in PagerDuty.
- [ ] Dependency graph registered in Enterprise Service Catalog.
- [ ] Toil budget capped at $< 50\%$ of squad time.
- [ ] Weekly operational review scheduled.
