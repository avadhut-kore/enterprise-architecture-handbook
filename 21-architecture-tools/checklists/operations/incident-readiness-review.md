# Checklist: Incident Readiness Review Checklist

## Executive Summary
This operational checklist must be validated prior to production promotion.

---

## Verification Criteria
- [ ] Severity matrix (SEV-1 to SEV-4) agreed across business and tech.
- [ ] Incident Commander role established with clear operational authority.
- [ ] War room Zoom/Slack automation configured for SEV-1 alerts.
- [ ] Blameless post-mortem process and PIR template published.
- [ ] External status page communication templates pre-approved.
