# Tool: Capability Mapping Worksheet

A structured markdown template for decomposing and cataloging enterprise business capabilities.

---

```markdown
### Capability Definition Worksheet
* **Capability ID**: CAP-[Domain]-[Number]
* **Capability Name**: [Noun + Verb / Gerund + Noun]
* **Parent Level-1 Capability**: [Parent Name]
* **Executive Business Sponsor**: [VP Name & Business Unit]
* **Lead Enterprise Architect**: [Architect Name]
* **Current Operational Maturity**: [1-Initial, 2-Repeatable, 3-Defined, 4-Automated, 5-Differentiating]
* **Target Maturity (24-Month)**: [Target Level]
* **Strategic Importance**: [Commodity, Necessary Core, Strategic Differentiator]
* **Supporting Applications**: [List Application IDs]
* **Core Data Entities**: [List Entities created/consumed]
```
