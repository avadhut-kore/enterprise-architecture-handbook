# Tool: Modernization Prioritization Calculator

Quantitative formula to rank legacy applications for modernization investment.

---

## 1. Formula
$$\text{Priority} = \frac{(\text{Urgency} \times 0.35) + (\text{Risk} \times 0.30) + (\text{ROI} \times 0.25)}{\text{Complexity} \times 0.10}$$

* Inputs are rated on a 1.0 to 10.0 scale.
* Applications scoring >7.5 are classified as **P0 (Immediate Capital Modernization)**.
