# Continuous Improvement Operational Checklist

> **"Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement. Regularly auditing your habits ensures your career trajectory is compounding upward rather than stagnating."**

---

## 1. Daily Cadence (15 Minutes)
- [ ] Framed the day: Selected 1 non-negotiable Most Important Task (MIT).
- [ ] Protected at least one 90-minute uninterrupted deep focus block.
- [ ] Kept PR reviews within the team's 18-hour turnaround SLA.
- [ ] Committed WIP cleanly and recorded friction in the daily log before shutdown.

---

## 2. Weekly Cadence (Friday 2–3 Hours)
- [ ] **PR Self-Audit**: Reviewed own merged pull requests from the past sprint; identified 1 area for craft improvement.
- [ ] **Technical Deep Reading**: Read 1 academic computer science paper, IETF RFC, or industry post-mortem.
- [ ] **Deliberate Sandbox Spike**: Built and benchmarked an isolated coding/concurrency/chaos challenge in a local sandbox.
- [ ] **Evidence Log**: Appended all notable PR diffs, ADRs, and incident links to the weekly evidence scratchpad.

---

## 3. Monthly Cadence (1–2 Hours)
- [ ] **Health Audit**: Completed the [Engineering Health Assessment Scorecard](../assessment/engineering-health-assessment.md); calculated Composite Health Index (CHI).
- [ ] **Telemetry Trends**: Reviewed 30-day error budget burn rates, P99 latency profiles, and cloud infrastructure bills.
- [ ] **Portfolio Curation**: Formatted the month's top 1–2 artifacts into Tier 3 CPOE entries in `portfolio.yaml`.
- [ ] **1-on-1 Career Sync**: Calibrated developmental progress and blind spots with the Tech Lead / Engineering Manager.

---

## 4. Quarterly Cadence (Half-Day)
- [ ] **90-Day Retrospective**: Scored the completed [90-Day Improvement Plan](../improvement-cycle/90-day-improvement-plan.md).
- [ ] **Re-Calibrate Baseline**: Re-assessed capability scores on the [Maturity Rubric](../capability-matrix/maturity-levels.md).
- [ ] **Gap Analysis**: Ran the [Capability Gap Analysis](../assessment/capability-gap-analysis.md) to identify the next bottleneck.
- [ ] **Formulate Next Plan**: Authored the upcoming 90-day improvement plan (1 primary + 1 secondary focus).
