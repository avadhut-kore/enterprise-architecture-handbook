# SRE Foundations & Core Philosophy

## Executive Summary

1. **Operations as a Software Problem**: SRE treats site operations as an engineering discipline. SREs build software systems to run architectures autonomously.
2. **Acceptable Failure**: 100% reliability is the wrong target for almost everything. Extreme reliability incurs exponential cost and halts innovation. The **Error Budget** represents the acceptable rate of failure tolerated by users.
3. **The 50% Rule**: SRE teams must cap operational toil (tickets, on-call pages, manual updates) at a maximum of 50% of their time; the remaining 50%+ must be dedicated to software engineering that permanently eliminates toil.
