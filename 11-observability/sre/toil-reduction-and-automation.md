# Toil Reduction & Operational Automation

## Executive Summary

Toil is operational work that is manual, repetitive, automatable, tactical, devoid of enduring value, and that scales linearly with service growth.

---

## Toil Elimination Strategy
1. **Identify**: Track weekly hours spent on password resets, certificate renewals, node recycling, and manual database exports.
2. **Automate**: Replace manual tasks with self-service APIs and Kubernetes operators.
3. **Measure**: Celebrate squads that permanently delete manual runbooks by codifying them into automated controllers.
