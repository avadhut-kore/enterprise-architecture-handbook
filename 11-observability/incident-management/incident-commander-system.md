# The Incident Commander (IC) System

## Executive Summary

During a major SEV-1 outage, standard engineering hierarchy is suspended. The **Incident Commander (IC)** holds supreme operational authority:
- **Incident Commander (IC)**: Directs the triage; assigns diagnostic tasks; prevents chaos; makes go/no-go rollback decisions.
- **Operations Lead**: Hands on keyboard; executes runbook diagnostics and rollbacks.
- **Communications Lead**: Isolates the technical team from executives; publishes regular internal and external status page updates every 15–30 minutes.
