# BCP vs DR vs Incident Management

## Executive Summary

Enterprise governance requires distinguishing between business survival frameworks and technical recovery procedures.

---

## 1. Domain Separation Matrix

| Discipline | Scope & Responsibility | Key Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Business Continuity Planning (BCP)**| Entire enterprise organization (People, facilities, suppliers, legal, payroll). | Business Continuity Plan, manual fallback procedures, regulatory notifications. |
| **Disaster Recovery (DR)** | Technical IT infrastructure and software platforms. | RTO/RPO targets, cross-region replication, automated failover runbooks. |
| **Crisis Management** | C-suite executive communications and public relations. | Press releases, board briefings, law enforcement engagement. |
| **Incident Management (SRE)**| Day-to-day operational triage of transient software degradations. | PagerDuty alerting, root cause analysis (RCA), blameless post-mortems. |
