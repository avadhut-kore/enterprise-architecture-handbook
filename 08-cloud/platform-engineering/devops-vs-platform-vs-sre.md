# Team Topologies: DevOps vs Platform Engineering vs SRE vs CCoE

## Executive Summary

Enterprise organizations frequently confuse operational roles. Clear team topology definitions prevent organizational friction.

---

## 1. Operational Roles Comparison Matrix

| Team / Role | Primary Mission | Primary Customer | Success Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stream-Aligned (DevOps)**| Build and operate specific business features | External Business Customers | Feature lead time, business revenue |
| **Platform Engineering** | Build self-service infrastructure platforms & Golden Paths| Internal Software Engineers | Developer cycle time, IDP Net Promoter Score (NPS) |
| **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)**| Protect system availability, latency, and operational health| End Users & Product Teams | Error budgets, SLO adherence, MTTR |
| **Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE)**| Define cloud governance, standards, and architecture strategy| Enterprise Leadership & ARB | Security compliance, cloud ROI, architectural maturity |
