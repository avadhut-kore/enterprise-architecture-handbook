# FinOps Principles & Organizational Lifecycle

## Executive Summary

FinOps shifts financial accountability from delayed monthly accounting reviews to real-time engineering decision-making.

---

## 1. Core FinOps Principles (FinOps Foundation)

1. **Teams Need to Collaborate**: Engineering, finance, and business teams work as a unified unit.
2. **Everyone Takes Ownership for Their Cloud Usage**: Software engineers own the financial cost of the infrastructure their code provisions.
3. **A Centralized Team Drives FinOps**: A central FinOps team governs discounting, commitment negotiations, and shared tooling.
4. **Reports Should Be Accessible and Timely**: Cloud cost data must be delivered to engineering teams in near real time, not 45 days later on a spreadsheet.
5. **Decisions are Driven by the Business Value of Cloud**: Cloud spend increases are celebrated if they drive proportionally greater business revenue.
