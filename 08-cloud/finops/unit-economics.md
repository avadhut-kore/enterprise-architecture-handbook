# Cloud Unit Economics: Linking Spend to Business Value

## Executive Summary

Raw cloud spend ($/month) is a meaningless metric in isolation. A company spending $\$100,000/\text{month}$ processing 1,000 transactions is failing; a company spending $\$1,000,000/\text{month}$ processing 100,000,000 transactions is exceptionally efficient. **Unit Economics** connects infrastructure cost directly to business transactions.

---

## 1. Enterprise Unit Economic Metrics

$$\text{Cloud Unit Cost} = \frac{\text{Total Cloud Spend for Service}}{\text{Total Business Output Metric}}$$

### Examples across Enterprise Sectors:
- **Financial Banking**: Infrastructure Cost per Settled Payment ($\$0.0024 / \text{transaction}$).
- **B2B SaaS**: Infrastructure Cost per Active Tenant per Month ($\$14.50 / \text{tenant}$).
- **E-Commerce**: Infrastructure Cost per Completed Order Checkout ($\$0.08 / \text{order}$).
- **Media Streaming**: Infrastructure Cost per Streamed Video Hour ($\$0.012 / \text{hour}$).

---

## 2. Architectural Value of Unit Economics
- When application refactoring reduces the cost per payment from $\$0.0030$ to $\$0.0015$, architects can demonstrate multi-million dollar annual savings directly to the CFO.
