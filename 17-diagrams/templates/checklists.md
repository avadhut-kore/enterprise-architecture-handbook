# Template Contribution & Quality Checklist

- [ ] Does the raw `.mmd` or `.puml` file render without compilation errors?
- [ ] Are wire protocols explicitly noted on all connection lines?
- [ ] Are all node names generic and architectural rather than specific to a single product?
- [ ] Is the template decoupled from proprietary hard-coded company IP?
- [ ] Are appropriate color styles (`classDef` or `skinparam`) integrated?
