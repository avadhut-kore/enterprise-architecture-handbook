# Enterprise Architecture Review Checklist

This checklist provides a structured 25-point evaluation for strategic enterprise architecture, capability alignment, and technology governance.

## 1. Business Capability & Strategy Alignment
- [ ] Does the proposed architecture directly trace to recognized enterprise business capabilities?
- [ ] Are redundant applications serving the same business capability eliminated or consolidated?
- [ ] Are business process handoffs across department boundaries clearly modeled?

## 2. Technology Radar & Standards Compliance
- [ ] Do all proposed programming languages, databases, and frameworks reside in the 'Adopt' or 'Trial' rings of the Technology Radar?
- [ ] Has an Architecture Exception Request been submitted for any technology residing in the 'Hold' or unvetted rings?
- [ ] Does the solution leverage shared enterprise platform capabilities (e.g., identity, transit network, observability)?

## 3. Application Portfolio & Lifecycle
- [ ] Is the application categorized under the Gartner TIME framework (Tolerate, Invest, Migrate, Eliminate)?
- [ ] Is total cost of ownership (TCO) calculated, including licensing, cloud hosting, and ongoing maintenance?
- [ ] Is a sunset and decommissioning plan established for replaced legacy components?
