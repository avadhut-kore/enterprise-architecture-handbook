# Platform Engineering & Internal Developer Platforms (IDP)

Platform engineering designs and builds toolchains and workflows that enable self-service capabilities for software engineering organizations in the cloud-native era.

## Contents

- [Platform as a Product](./platform-as-a-product.md) — Treating internal developers as customers, developer personas, product roadmaps, and adoption metrics.
- [Internal Developer Platform Architecture](./internal-developer-platform-architecture.md) — Reference architecture (Developer Portal / Backstage, Platform API, Orchestration, Infrastructure Engine).
- [Golden Paths and Service Templates](./golden-paths-and-service-templates.md) — Designing opinionated paved roads, scaffolding tools, and optionality.
- [Platform SLOs and Governance](./platform-slos-and-governance.md) — Reliability targets, support tiers, and platform lifecycle management.

## Core Philosophy
The mission of a platform team is not to enforce rules or manage ticket queues. Its mission is to **reduce cognitive load and make the right architectural choice the easiest choice**.
