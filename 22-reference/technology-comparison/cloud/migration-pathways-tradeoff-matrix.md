# Technology Comparison: Migration Pathways Trade-Off Matrix

## Executive Summary
This reference matrix evaluates the architectural trade-offs between rehost vs replatform vs refactor.

---

## Architectural Comparison Matrix

| Dimension | Rehost (Lift & Shift) | Replatform (Lift & Reshape) | Refactor (Cloud-Native Rewrite) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Migration Timeline** | Weeks | Months | 6–18 Months |
| **Upfront Investment** | Low | Moderate | High |
| **Modernization ROI** | Low (Preserves technical debt) | High (Managed DB / Containers) | Maximum (Agility & Elasticity) |
| **Business Disruption** | Minimal | Low | High |
