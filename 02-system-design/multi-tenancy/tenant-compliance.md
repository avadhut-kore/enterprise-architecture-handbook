# Tenant Compliance & Data Sovereignty

## 1. Geo-Fencing & Data Residency
* **GDPR (European Union)**: Data must physically reside in EU data centers (e.g., `eu-central-1`).
* **HIPAA (Healthcare)**: Business Associate Agreements (BAA) and immutable audit logs.
* **Cell Routing**: Route requests from European tenants to an EU-only compute/database cell.
