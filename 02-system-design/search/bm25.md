# Okapi BM25 Ranking Algorithm

## 1. The Industry Standard (Lucene / Elasticsearch)
Okapi BM25 improves on classic TF-IDF by incorporating **Term Frequency Saturation** and **Document Length Normalization**:

$$\text{Score}(D, Q) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{IDF}(q_i) \cdot \frac{f(q_i, D) \cdot (k_1 + 1)}{f(q_i, D) + k_1 \cdot \left(1 - b + b \cdot \frac{|D|}{\text{avgdl}}\right)}$$

Where:
* $k_1 \approx 1.2$: Controls term frequency saturation (prevents repeating a word 100 times from boosting score infinitely).
* $b \approx 0.75$: Controls document length normalization (short titles penalized less than long articles).
