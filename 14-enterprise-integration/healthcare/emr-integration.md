# Departmental EMR Integration (PACS, LIS, RIS, Pharmacy)

## 1. Departmental Clinical Subsystems
A hospital environment encompasses specialized departmental systems:
- **LIS (Laboratory Information System)**: Manages blood/tissue pathology (`HL7 ORU^R01`).
- **RIS (Radiology Information System)**: Manages exam scheduling and reports (`HL7 ORM^O01`).
- **PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication System)**: Stores multi-gigabyte medical imaging files using DICOM protocol.
- **Pharmacy System**: Dispenses medications, checks contraindications (`HL7 RDE^O11`).
