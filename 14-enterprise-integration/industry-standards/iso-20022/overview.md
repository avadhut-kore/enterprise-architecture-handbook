# ISO 20022 Global Standards Overview

## 1. The Global Convergence
Global financial infrastructure is migrating from legacy fixed-width and proprietary formats (SWIFT MT, NACHA, FedLine) to ISO 20022 XML. 

### Why ISO 20022?
- **Rich, Structured Remittance Data**: Eliminates truncated invoice data; allows full invoice metadata to travel with the funds.
- **Enhanced AML / Sanctions Screening**: Structured party identifiers (LEI, BIC, full street addresses) minimize false-positive sanctions alerts.
- **Universal Financial Syntax**: Bridges domestic clearing rails and cross-border SWIFT payments seamlessly.
