# Architecture Discovery Questions Checklist

The master reusable interview checklist used during initial project discovery workshops.

---

### Phase 1: Business Motivation Discovery
- [ ] Primary business outcome documented (Revenue, Cost Reduction, Compliance, Risk).
- [ ] Cost of inaction quantified in monetary terms.
- [ ] Core business stakeholders and decision authorities identified.
- [ ] Customer journey touchpoints and value stream stages mapped.

### Phase 2: Technical & Non-Functional Discovery
- [ ] Peak throughput (requests/sec) and latency targets (p95/p99) documented.
- [ ] Availability SLA target (e.g., 99.95%) and downtime penalty mapped.
- [ ] RTO and RPO disaster recovery budgets defined.
- [ ] Data volume, growth rate, and archival retention periods quantified.

### Phase 3: Constraints & Boundary Discovery
- [ ] Hard regulatory and legal compliance constraints listed.
- [ ] Legacy system integration points and protocols (REST, SOAP, Mainframe, EDI) mapped.
- [ ] Fixed delivery deadlines and capital budget limits confirmed.
- [ ] Organizational skill gaps and team topology constraints recorded.
