# Architect Career Paths and Professional Growth

The software engineering career ladder branches at the Staff level. The architect path trades direct hands-on coding throughput for organizational leverage, strategic clarity, and high-stakes technical decision quality.

## 1. The Sphere of Influence Progression

```
Senior Engineer      ──► Sphere of Influence: Single Feature / Single Service
                         (Focus: Code quality, unit testing, delivery velocity)
                              │
                              ▼
Staff / Lead Arch    ──► Sphere of Influence: Single Team / Bounded Domain
                         (Focus: Subsystem architecture, performance, ADRs)
                              │
                              ▼
Principal Architect  ──► Sphere of Influence: Multi-Team Domain / Division
                         (Focus: Cross-system integration, platform strategy, RFCs)
                              │
                              ▼
Enterprise / Chief   ──► Sphere of Influence: Entire Enterprise / Boardroom
                         (Focus: Business capability strategy, M&A, FinOps, Risk)
```

## 2. Essential Habits of Master Architects
1. **Write Plainly and Concisely**: Master the art of the 1-page executive memo and concise RFC.
2. **Stay Grounded**: Spend at least 10% of your time running code, reading production post-mortems, or shadowing on-call rotations.
3. **Seek High-Leverage Problems**: Avoid getting dragged into trivial styling debates; focus where mistakes cost millions.
