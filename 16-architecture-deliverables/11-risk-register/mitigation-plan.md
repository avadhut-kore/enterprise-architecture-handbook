# Risk Treatment Strategies
* **Avoid**: Redesign architecture to eliminate the risk entirely (e.g., eliminate distributed transactions).
* **Mitigate**: Implement technical controls to reduce likelihood or impact (e.g., add circuit breakers, follower reads).
* **Transfer**: Offload risk to a third party (e.g., cloud SLA, cyber insurance).
* **Accept**: Formally acknowledge residual risk with ARB and executive sponsor sign-off.
