# Quantitative Risk Scoring Rubric

## Likelihood Scale (1 to 5)
1. **Rare**: Unlikely to occur (< 5% probability during lifecycle).
2. **Unlikely**: May occur in exceptional circumstances (5% - 20%).
3. **Possible**: Might occur during standard operations (20% - 50%).
4. **Likely**: Highly probable to occur without intervention (50% - 80%).
5. **Almost Certain**: Expected to occur (> 80% probability).

## Impact Scale (1 to 5)
1. **Insignificant**: Negligible technical debt; zero customer impact.
2. **Minor**: Localized non-critical service disruption; workarounds available.
3. **Moderate**: Measurable degradation of SLOs; noticeable customer impact.
4. **Major**: Extended Tier-1 system outage; financial penalties.
5. **Catastrophic**: Critical data loss, regulatory sanction, or severe brand damage.
