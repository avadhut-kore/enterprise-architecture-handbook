# Standard 60-Minute ARB Session Agenda

| Time | Agenda Item | Presenter | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00 - 00:05 | Welcome, Scope & Objective | ARB Chair | Establish review context and decision scope |
| 00:05 - 00:20 | Architecture Walkthrough | Solution Architect | Present business context, C4 diagrams, and core design |
| 00:20 - 00:35 | Key Trade-offs & ADR Review | Tech Lead | Discuss evaluated options and accepted compromises |
| 00:35 - 00:50 | Cross-Cutting Q&A (Sec, Data, Cloud) | ARB Panel | Probe resilience, security, cost, and operational risks |
| 00:50 - 01:00 | Closed Board Deliberation & Decision | ARB Members | Vote on outcome (Approved, Conditions, Rework) |
