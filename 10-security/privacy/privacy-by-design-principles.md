# Privacy by Design Architecture Principles

## Executive Summary

1. **Proactive not Reactive; Preventative not Remedial**: Privacy risks must be anticipated and prevented before they occur.
2. **Privacy as the Default Setting**: No action required by the individual to protect their privacy; built into the system by default.
3. **Full Functionality — Positive-Sum, not Zero-Sum**: Avoid false trade-offs (e.g., Privacy vs Security; both can and must be achieved).
