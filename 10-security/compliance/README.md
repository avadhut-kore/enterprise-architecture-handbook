# Compliance & Regulatory Architecture (`compliance/`)

## Executive Summary

Compliance architecture translates legal and regulatory mandates (GDPR, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001) into concrete technical designs, architectural controls, and automated audit evidence.
