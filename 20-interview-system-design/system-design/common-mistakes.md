# Common System Design Interview Pitfalls

## 1. The Top 10 Red Flags in Architect Interviews

```
+----+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| #  | Red Flag Trap                      | What the Interviewer Thinks              |
+----+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| 1  | Jumping straight to drawing boxes  | Lacks structured thinking; chaotic       |
| 2  | Silent drawing (no verbal stream)  | Poor communication; uncollaborative      |
| 3  | Buzzword bingo without rationale   | Cargo-cult engineer; shallow knowledge   |
| 4  | Ignoring interviewer hints/feedback| Stubborn; un-coachable                   |
| 5  | Unbounded single points of failure | Inexperienced in production operations   |
| 6  | Missing scale math completely      | Guesses capacity; cannot size production |
| 7  | Pretending trade-offs don't exist  | Dogmatic; lacks architectural maturity   |
| 8  | Designing for 10 users, not 100M   | Doesn't understand distributed systems   |
| 9  | Over-engineering simple MVPs       | Accidental complexity enthusiast         |
| 10 | Running out of time on step 1      | Poor time management; lacks leadership   |
+----+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
```
