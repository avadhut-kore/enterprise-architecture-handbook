## 2. Standard 19-Section Case Study Structure
Every case study in this directory conforms strictly to the enterprise post-mortem standard:
1. Context | 2. Business Problem | 3. Organization | 4. Current Architecture | 5. Business Capabilities | 6. Constraints | 7. NFRs | 8. Architecture Options | 9. Decision | 10. Target Architecture | 11. Transition Architecture | 12. Roadmap | 13. Governance | 14. Risks | 15. Cost | 16. Trade-offs | 17. Lessons Learned | 18. Alternative Decisions | 19. Related Architecture Patterns.
