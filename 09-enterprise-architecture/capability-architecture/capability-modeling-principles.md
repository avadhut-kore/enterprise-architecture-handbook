# Business Capability Modeling Principles

Rules and guardrails for constructing enterprise business capability maps that endure over decades.

---

## 1. The 6 Mandatory Capability Modeling Rules

1. **Focus on 'WHAT', Never 'HOW'**:
   * *Bad (Process/Implementation)*: "Scan PDF invoices using Tesseract OCR and email approval."
   * *Good (Capability)*: "Accounts Payable Invoice Processing."
2. **Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive (MECE)**:
   * Capabilities must not overlap functionally. A single activity belongs to exactly one capability.
3. **Independent of Organizational Structure**:
   * Do NOT model capabilities after departments. If "Human Resources" and "Global Marketing" both purchase recruitment software, the capability is "Talent Acquisition," regardless of who operates it.
4. **Standardized Naming Convention**:
   * Always use **Noun + Verb** or **Gerund Verb + Noun** (e.g., *Customer Identity Verification*, *Payment Settlement*, *Portfolio Rebalancing*). Never use technology acronyms or vendor brand names.
5. **Technology Agnostic**:
   * A capability exists even if the organization executes it with pencils and paper. Technology enables the capability; it is not the capability.
6. **Limit Depth to 3 Levels**:
   * Stop at Level 3. Level 4 invariably degenerates into business process steps and software implementation specifications.
