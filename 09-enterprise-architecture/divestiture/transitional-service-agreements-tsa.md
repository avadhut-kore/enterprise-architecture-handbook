# Transitional Service Agreements (TSA) Architecture

Architecting and governing IT services provided to a divested company during the post-sale transition window.

---

## 1. The TSA Operational Model
* **Definition**: A legal contract where the parent company continues to host and operate shared IT services (ERP, email, payroll, network) for the divested unit for 6–24 months post-closing.
* **Architectural Risk**: Parent enterprise remains legally liable for system outages and cybersecurity incidents affecting the buyer's new subsidiary.
* **Financial Penalties**: TSAs carry aggressive rate escalations (e.g., Year 1: Cost + 15%; Year 2: Cost + 75%) to incentivize the buyer to exit the parent infrastructure rapidly.
