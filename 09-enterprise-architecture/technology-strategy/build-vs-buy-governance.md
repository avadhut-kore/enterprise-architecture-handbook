# Build vs Buy Governance Framework

A quantitative decision framework to determine whether an enterprise capability should be built in-house or purchased as commercial SaaS.

---

## 1. The Core Build vs Buy Decision Matrix

| Evaluation Dimension | Weight | Score (1–5): Favor BUILD | Score (1–5): Favor BUY / SaaS |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Strategic Differentiation** | 0.25 | **5**: Provides unique competitive advantage in the market. | **1**: Commodity business process (payroll, CRM, ERP). |
| **Market Availability of SaaS** | 0.20 | **5**: No commercial software satisfies unique requirements. | **1**: Mature, established SaaS leaders dominate market. |
| **Total Cost of Ownership (5-Yr)** | 0.20 | **5**: Building in-house is demonstrably cheaper at enterprise scale. | **1**: High engineering build & ongoing maintenance cost. |
| **Internal Engineering Capacity** | 0.15 | **5**: Deep world-class internal domain expertise. | **1**: Scarce engineering talent; high hiring friction. |
| **Customization & Control Need** | 0.10 | **5**: Requires continuous rapid daily proprietary tweaking. | **1**: Standard industry workflows are fully acceptable. |
| **Time-to-Market Urgency** | 0.10 | **1**: 18-month build timeline acceptable. | **5**: Urgent business need; turnkey deployment in 30 days. |

---

## 2. The Golden Rule
> **"Buy commodity capabilities to run the business; Build proprietary capabilities that differentiate the business."**
* *Buy*: HR payroll, employee expense management, standard CRM, corporate email.
* *Build*: Core proprietary algorithmic pricing, fraud detection, high-frequency matching engine.
