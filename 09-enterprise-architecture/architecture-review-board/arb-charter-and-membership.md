# Architecture Review Board: Charter & Membership

The formal governance charter empowering the Architecture Review Board.

---

## 1. ARB Membership Composition
* **Chair**: Chief Architect (holds tie-breaking vote).
* **Permanent Voting Members**:
  * Lead Enterprise Architect (Core Platforms)
  * Lead Business Architect
  * Enterprise Data Architect
  * Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) or Delegate
  * Lead Cloud & Infrastructure Architect
* **Rotating Members**:
  * Domain Architect of the presenting business unit
  * Solution Architect and Engineering Lead of the project under review

---

## 2. Review Thresholds (When Must an Initiative Go to the ARB?)
A project must appear before the ARB if it meets **any** of the following criteria:
1. **Capital Budget**: IT expenditure exceeds $1.0M Capex.
2. **Criticality**: System classified as Tier-0 or Tier-1 (Mission Critical).
3. **New Technology**: Proposes introducing a new technology not currently marked "Adopt" or "Trial" on the enterprise Technology Radar.
4. **Data Risk**: Processes, stores, or transmits Restricted PII, PCI-DSS cardholder data, or healthcare records.
5. **Cross-Domain Integration**: Integrates across multiple distinct business unit boundaries.
