# Business Architecture Checklist

- [ ] Capability boundaries are MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive).
- [ ] Capabilities named with standard Noun-Verb conventions (technology-agnostic).
- [ ] Executive business owners identified for all Level-1 and Level-2 capabilities.
- [ ] Flow efficiency calculated across core customer value streams.
- [ ] Capability heatmaps updated to reflect current technical health and strategic urgency.
