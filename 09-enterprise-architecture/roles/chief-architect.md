# Role Profile: The Chief Architect

The Chief Architect is the executive technical authority within an enterprise, reporting directly to the CIO, CTO, or Global Head of Engineering.

---

## 1. Executive Mandate
The Chief Architect owns the overall enterprise architecture strategy, leads the global architecture guild/practice, and acts as the ultimate escalation point for architectural disputes across the enterprise.

### Core Responsibilities:
1. **Executive Alignment**: Translate board-level corporate strategy into actionable multi-year technology roadmaps.
2. **Global Architecture Governance**: Establish and enforce the enterprise architecture charter, decision rights, and ARB authority across all business units and subsidiaries.
3. **M&A Technical Due Diligence**: Lead technical assessments of acquisition targets, estimating integration complexity, technical debt liabilities, and platform synergy savings.
4. **Talent & Practice Leadership**: Recruit, mentor, and elevate the enterprise, solution, and technical architecture talent pool across the company.
5. **Vendor & Partner Negotiations**: Partner with procurement to architect multi-year, multi-million dollar master services agreements (MSAs) with cloud providers and enterprise software vendors.

---

## 2. Chief Architect Decision Rights
* **Final Authority on Standards**: Approves enterprise-wide technology standards (e.g., corporate cloud provider, standard ERP/CRM platform, core database engines).
* **Veto Power on Non-Compliant Investments**: Holds formal authority to block capital projects that violate critical architecture, security, or data sovereignty policies.
* **Exception Sign-off**: Final approver for Tier-1 architectural exceptions involving multi-million dollar or enterprise-risk exposure.
