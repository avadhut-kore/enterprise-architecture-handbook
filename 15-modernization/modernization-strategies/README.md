# The 11 Rs Enterprise Modernization Strategies

## 1. Overview
The **11 Rs Modernization Framework** is an architectural decision model that categorizes the viable strategies for evolving an existing enterprise software system. It expands upon the traditional Gartner "5 Rs" and cloud hyperscaler "6 Rs" to address complex enterprise realities, including SaaS repurchase, code-level refactoring, and active system retention.

## 2. The 11 Strategies Catalog
1. [retain.md](retain.md): **Retain** (Do Nothing / Active Encapsulation) — Keeping stable, high-ROI legacy systems intact.
2. [retire.md](retire.md): **Retire** (Decommission) — System shutdown, legal data archiving, and license termination.
3. [replace-repurchase.md](replace-repurchase.md): **Replace & Repurchase** — Sunsetting custom software in favor of commercial SaaS or COTS platforms.
4. [rehost.md](rehost.md): **Rehost** (Lift and Shift) — Moving workloads as-is to cloud IaaS without architectural modification.
5. [relocate.md](relocate.md): **Relocate** (Hypervisor Shift) — Shifting live virtualized environments (e.g., VMware to AWS/Azure VMware Solution).
6. [replatform.md](replatform.md): **Replatform** (Lift, Tinker, and Shift) — Upgrading runtimes, adopting managed databases, or containerizing.
7. [refactor.md](refactor.md): **Refactor** (Code Modernization) — Improving internal non-functional code quality while preserving behavior.
8. [rearchitect.md](rearchitect.md): **Rearchitect** (Cloud-Native Redesign) — Decomposing monoliths into microservices, event-driven, or modular monolith architectures.
9. [rewrite-rebuild.md](rewrite-rebuild.md): **Rewrite & Rebuild** — Rebuilding functionality from scratch on cloud-native primitives.
10. [strategy-comparison-matrix.md](strategy-comparison-matrix.md): **Comparison Matrix** — Comparative trade-offs across cost, risk, duration, and reversibility.
11. [strategy-selection-guide.md](strategy-selection-guide.md): **Selection Guide** — Decision trees, scoring rubrics, and organizational alignment.
