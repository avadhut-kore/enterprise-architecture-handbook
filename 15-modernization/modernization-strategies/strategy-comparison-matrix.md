# Modernization Strategy Comparative Matrix

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Evaluation Matrix

| Strategy | Speed to Value | Initial CapEx | Ongoing OpEx | Risk Profile | Disruption to Business | Reversibility | Long-Term Strategic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Retain** | Immediate | $0 | High (Legacy maintenance) | Very Low | Zero | High | Low (Preserves status quo) |
| **Retire** | High | Low | $0 (Savings) | Low | Low | Moderate | High (Eliminates debt) |
| **Replace (SaaS)** | Medium | Medium | Medium (Subscription) | Medium | High (Process change) | Low | High (Shifts ops to vendor)|
| **Rehost** | High | Medium | High (Unoptimized cloud) | Low | Low | High | Low (Lacks cloud benefits) |
| **Relocate** | Very High | Low | High (Dedicated cloud) | Very Low | Minimal | Very High | Low (Same architecture) |
| **Replatform** | Medium | Medium | Medium (Managed services) | Medium | Low | High | Medium (Reduced ops debt) |
| **Refactor** | Medium | Medium | Low (Efficient compute) | Medium | Low | High | High (Maintainable code) |
| **Rearchitect**| Low | High | Low to Medium | High | High | Moderate | Very High (Maximum agility)|
| **Rewrite** | Very Low | Very High | Low to Medium | Very High | Extreme | Very Low | Extreme (Clean slate / High risk)|
| **Rebuild** | Low | High | Low (Serverless/PaaS) | High | High | Low | High (Cloud-native efficiency)|
| **Repurchase** | Medium | Medium | Medium (SaaS) | Medium | Medium | Low | High (Vendor managed) |
