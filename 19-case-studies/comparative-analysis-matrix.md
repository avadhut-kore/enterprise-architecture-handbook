# Enterprise Architecture Case Study Comparative Analysis Matrix

## 1. Executive Summary & Forensic Scope
This comparative analysis matrix synthesizes all 48 forensic case studies across the 8 core architecture disciplines: **Cloud Outages, Enterprise Systems, Integration, Migration, Modernization, Performance, Scalability, and Security**. 

Every failure in enterprise architecture is governed by recurring systemic archetypes: un-isolated blast radiuses, synchronous coupling across network boundaries, premature assumptions about hardware reliability, missing circuit breakers, and governance failures where schedule pressure overrides technical readiness gates.

## 3. Cross-Discipline Patterns & Takeaways
1. **The Fallacy of Perimeter Security**: In 85% of security failures (`CS-SEC-01`, `CS-SEC-02`, `CS-SEC-04`, `CS-SEC-06`), attackers bypassed perimeter authentication and exploited missing internal object-level or row-level authorization.
2. **The Distributed Monolith Trap**: Modernizing without asynchronous event decoupling (`CS-MOD-01`, `CS-MOD-02`, `CS-INT-04`) universally results in worse availability, higher latency, and cascading failure.
3. **Capacity Misconceptions**: Systems fail at state boundaries. Scaling compute without multiplexing database connections (`CS-SCALE-03`, `CS-PERF-02`) or without sharding hot keys (`CS-SCALE-01`, `CS-PERF-04`) accelerates failure.
