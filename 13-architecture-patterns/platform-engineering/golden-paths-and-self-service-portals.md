# Golden Paths & Self-Service Governance

## 1. Paved Roads vs. Mandatory Guardrails

A **Golden Path** is an opinionated, supported, and fully-automated route to production. 

Teams that adopt the Golden Path receive automated security patching, pre-configured CI/CD pipelines, integrated observability, and instant compliance approval. Teams choosing alternative architectures must assume complete operational ownership and undergo rigorous manual Architecture Review Board (ARB) audits.
