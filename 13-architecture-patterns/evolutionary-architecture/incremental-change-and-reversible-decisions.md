# Incremental Change & Reversible Decisions

## 1. Two-Way Doors vs. One-Way Doors

* **Type 1 (One-Way Doors / Irreversible)**: Relational vs. Document database migration; selecting a primary cloud provider; core programming language baseline. Require exhaustive ARB review and prototypes.
* **Type 2 (Two-Way Doors / Reversible)**: Choosing between two AI model providers behind a Model Gateway; switching chunking sizes; adjusting caching TTLs. Can be made rapidly by autonomous squads without ARB approval.
