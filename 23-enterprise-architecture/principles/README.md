# Enterprise Architecture Principles

Enterprise Architecture Principles are enduring, consensus-based rules that guide the design, procurement, and deployment of technology assets across an entire enterprise.

---

## 1. The 7-Part Principle Format Standard

Every principle in this catalog strictly follows the enterprise specification standard:
1. **Principle Statement**: Clear, unambiguous directive.
2. **Why**: The underlying business motivation.
3. **Rationale**: The technical and strategic justification.
4. **Implications**: The operational, cost, and design consequences.
5. **Exceptions**: Specific, bounded conditions where deviation is allowed.
6. **Example**: Concrete production implementation scenario.
7. **Anti-pattern**: Common violation and resulting organizational failure.
