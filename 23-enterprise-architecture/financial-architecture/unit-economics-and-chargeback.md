# Unit Economics & Chargeback Models

Connecting IT spending directly to the business units and products that generate corporate revenue.

---

## 1. Showback vs Chargeback

| Model | Mechanism | Behavioral Impact | Best Suited For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Overhead (Allocation)** | IT costs pooled and divided by company headcount. | Toxic: Business units view IT as a free, unlimited resource; no incentive to optimize. | Early-stage organizations (<500 employees). |
| **Showback** | Business units receive monthly itemized bills showing exact cloud and software spend, but no money changes hands. | High: Creates awareness and public benchmarking without complex accounting friction. | Mid-maturity organizations transitioning to FinOps. |
| **Chargeback** | Actual inter-departmental financial journal transfers occur; IT spend deducts directly from business unit P&L. | Maximum: Product managers aggressively right-size infrastructure and eliminate unused SaaS seats. | Highly mature digital enterprises. |
