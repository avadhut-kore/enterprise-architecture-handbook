# Transition Architecture Design Principles

Why the journey matters more than the destination in enterprise architecture.

---

## 1. Rules for Transition Plateau Architecture

1. **The Plateau Invariant**: Each plateau must be an architecturally stable state where the organization can pause indefinitely without operational decay.
2. **Dual-Running Economics**: Dual-running legacy and modern systems simultaneously inflates operating costs; transition phases must be aggressively time-boxed (max 6–12 months per plateau).
3. **Data Reversibility**: Always maintain bi-directional data synchronization during cutover so traffic can be instantly routed back to legacy in the event of an unpredicted production anomaly.
