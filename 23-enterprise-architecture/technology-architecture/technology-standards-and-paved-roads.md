# Technology Standards & Paved Roads

Balancing engineering innovation with enterprise standardization through curated Golden Paths.

---

## 1. The 3 Tiers of Technology Standards

| Tier | Policy | Architectural Stance | Engineering Team Rights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Paved Road (Standard)** | Fully supported enterprise golden path with automated CI/CD, templates, and 24/7 platform team support. | Strongly Encouraged | Zero friction, zero ARB review required. |
| **Tier 2: Tolerated (Secondary)** | Permitted for specialized use cases (e.g., Go for high-throughput network proxies). | Allowed with Justification | Light ARB review; team must provide their own operational support. |
| **Tier 3: Prohibited (Blacklisted)** | Obsolete, vulnerable, or non-compliant technologies (e.g., PHP 5, unsupported Cassandra, unvetted open source). | Strictly Forbidden | Blocked automatically in CI/CD pipeline. Requires Chief Architect waiver. |
