# Role Profile: The Solution Architect

The Solution Architect (SA) designs end-to-end technical solutions to address specific business problems, translating enterprise architecture guidelines and business requirements into viable, production-ready system designs.

---

## 1. Key Accountabilities
* **Solution Architecture Document (SAD)**: Author comprehensive solution blueprints including C4 context, container, and component diagrams.
* **Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)**: Define and validate scalability, availability, security, performance, and disaster recovery SLA/SLO budgets.
* **Enterprise Alignment**: Ensure the solution complies with enterprise technology standards, approved reference architectures, and security policies.
* **Delivery Squad Guidance**: Partner with engineering leads, technical architects, and product managers to resolve technical ambiguities during implementation.
* **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)**: Document significant architectural choices, evaluated alternatives, and accepted trade-offs.

---

## 2. Solution Architect vs Enterprise Architect

| Dimension | Solution Architect (SA) | Enterprise Architect (EA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope** | Single initiative, product line, or system solution. | Cross-enterprise, multi-system, multi-year portfolio. |
| **Primary Goal** | Deliver a functioning, compliant, resilient system. | Maximize business capability value, eliminate redundancy, minimize TCO. |
| **Depth** | Medium-to-deep across application, data, API, and cloud deployment. | Broad across business strategy, capability mapping, operating models, and technology standards. |
| **Output** | High-level design (HLD), interface contracts, cloud topologies. | Capability heatmaps, enterprise roadmaps, technology radars, governance charters. |
