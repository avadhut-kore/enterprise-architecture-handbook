# Presenting to the C-Suite: CIO, CTO, CFO, and Board

A practical playbook for structuring architecture presentations to senior corporate executives.

---

## 1. Executive Audience Personas

| Role | Primary Concerns | What Wins Them Over | Fatal Mistakes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CIO** | Strategic alignment, delivery predictability, vendor risk, operational stability. | Demonstrating how architecture accelerates business roadmap while reducing system outages. | Deep technical jargon; ignoring operational support realities. |
| **CTO** | Technical scalability, engineering productivity, talent retention, modern platform reuse. | Showing elegant technical blueprints, self-service developer platforms, and reduced cognitive load. | Hand-waving diagrams without concrete scalability or latency analysis. |
| **CFO** | Capital efficiency, payback period, TCO reduction, licensing rationalization. | Quantified financial ROI, software consolidation savings, clear Capex vs Opex modeling. | Discussing technical elegance; inability to explain financial payback. |
| **CISO** | Data protection, regulatory compliance, Zero Trust, supply chain vulnerabilities. | Security-by-design, automated compliance gates, explicit threat modeling. | Treating security as an afterthought or an exception waiver. |

---

## 2. The 5-Slide Executive Architecture Deck Structure

* **Slide 1: Executive Summary & Recommendation** (Governing thought, capital requested, business outcome delivered).
* **Slide 2: The Strategic Problem & Cost of Inaction** (Business pain, quantified financial/operational risk).
* **Slide 3: Proposed Architecture & Target State** (Clean high-level block diagram showing business capabilities).
* **Slide 4: Phased Transition Roadmap & Milestones** (Quarterly delivery plateaus, quick wins, de-risking strategy).
* **Slide 5: Financials, Trade-offs & Risks** (TCO comparison, alternative options rejected, risk mitigation matrix).
